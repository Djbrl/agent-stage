How the live demo works

  1. It runs on our own laptop. The app is served from a MacBook Pro (M1) in Dakar, through a secure tunnel. If the laptop is asleep, the demo is offline.
  2. You get a private sandbox. Pressing "Try it live" starts a fresh Docker container just for you, with two small sample projects. The agent really creates files, runs tests and opens pages in a browser, but only inside your sandbox. It has no internet access and is deleted 20 minutes after you leave.
  3. Real models. Claude Sonnet 5.5 does the work, in our own agent loop or through the Claude Code engine. During tests our open model (Qwen3-Coder, on an NVIDIA L40S) is on too. If neither is available, a scripted demo model plays a fixed example. The status next to the button tells you what you'll get.
  4. You're in charge. Commands wait for your approval, and every task can be undone.
You can
Create, edit and delete files; run commands, Node, Python, pytest and git; start a local web server and open it in the agent's browser; use Guide me, Just build it, Ask why and Undo.
You can't
Reach the internet from the sandbox (no npm/pip installs, no cloning, no outside websites), reach our laptop or other visitors, or change keys or settings.
Limits
1 CPU core, 1.5 GB RAM, 512 MB of temporary space, 1 GB of disk (a sandbox that writes more is deleted), 400k model tokens and $5 of Claude per sandbox, 5 sandboxes at a time.
