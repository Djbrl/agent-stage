# Code of Conduct

Codetandem follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text there applies; this page is a short adaptation of it.

## Our pledge
We want taking part in Codetandem to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This is a project for learners. Beginner questions are welcome, and nobody should be made to feel small for asking one.

## What we expect
- Be kind and respectful, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Keep the discussion focused on what is best for the people using and building Codetandem.

## What is not acceptable
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope
This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, the online demo) and when someone represents
the project in public.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Jib (Djbrl), at **sydjbrl@gmail.com**. Every report will be read and
answered promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the
Contributor Covenant.
